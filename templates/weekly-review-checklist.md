# Weekly Review Checklist
TrollProof Playbook – Monitoring & Adaptation

**Purpose:** 15–20 minute weekly ritual to stay ahead of patent risks, maintain velocity, and strengthen moats — without paranoia or lawyer bills.
Run every Friday (or Sunday reset). Keeps you proactive, not reactive.

**Time Estimate:** 15–20 minutes
**Tools Needed:** This checklist, your calendar/notion, Google Alerts dashboard, USPTO watch (optional).
