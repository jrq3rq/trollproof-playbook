# NDA for Consultants / Contractors Template
TrollProof Playbook – Trade Secrets Protection

**Purpose:** Slightly stronger one-page NDA tailored for freelancers, consultants, developers, or advisors who may access more sensitive proprietary info (e.g., AI prompts, model architecture details, user data pipelines, internal roadmaps). Still simple and founder-friendly.

**Important notes:**
- Not lawyer-drafted or jurisdiction-specific.
- Use for medium-risk engagements (e.g., part-time dev, design consultant, early advisor).
- For full-time hires, long-term partners, or high-stakes IP, get a proper attorney-drafted mutual NDA.
- Sign digitally. Keep signed copies private + timestamped.
- Customize brackets [] before use.

---

**NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (Consultant / Contractor – One-Way)**

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on [DATE], by and between:

- **Disclosing Party:** [YOUR FULL NAME or COMPANY NAME], located at [YOUR CITY, STATE, COUNTRY] ("Discloser")
- **Receiving Party:** [CONSULTANT FULL NAME or COMPANY NAME], located at [CONSULTANT CITY, STATE, COUNTRY] ("Recipient")

**1. Definition of Confidential Information**
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by Discloser to Recipient in connection with the consulting services, whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by inspection, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential. This includes, but is not limited to:
- Source code, AI prompts, training methodologies, model weights/configs
- Proprietary algorithms, data processing pipelines, user behavior insights
- Business plans, product roadmaps, pricing strategies, customer lists
- Internal tools, prototypes, unreleased features
- Any information marked "Confidential" or disclosed under circumstances implying confidentiality

Confidential Information does **not** include information that:
(a) becomes publicly known through no fault of Recipient;
(b) was rightfully known to Recipient prior to disclosure (with written proof);
(c) is independently developed by Recipient without reference to Discloser's information;
(d) is lawfully obtained from a third party without confidentiality restriction.

**2. Obligations of Recipient**
Recipient agrees to:
(a) Maintain the Confidential Information in strict confidence and not disclose it to any third party without Discloser's prior written consent.
(b) Use the Confidential Information **solely** for performing the consulting services agreed upon and for no other purpose.
(c) Restrict access to Confidential Information to only those of Recipient's employees, subcontractors, or agents who have a legitimate need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those herein.
(d) Protect the Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care Recipient uses for its own confidential information of similar importance (but in no event less than reasonable care).
(e) Promptly notify Discloser in writing of any actual or suspected unauthorized use or disclosure.
(f) Not reverse-engineer, decompile, disassemble, or attempt to derive the source code, structure, or ideas behind any Confidential Information.

**3. Term and Survival**
This Agreement takes effect on the date first written above and continues for [CHOOSE ONE: 3 years / 5 years / the duration of the consulting relationship plus 2 years thereafter].
Obligations regarding trade secrets survive indefinitely as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

**4. Return or Destruction**
Upon termination of the consulting relationship, or upon Discloser's earlier written request, Recipient will promptly:
(a) Return or destroy all Confidential Information (including all copies, notes, extracts); and
(b) Certify in writing to Discloser that such return or destruction has been completed.

**5. No License or Obligation**
Nothing in this Agreement grants Recipient any license, ownership, or other rights to the Confidential Information. This Agreement does not obligate Discloser to provide any information or enter into any further relationship.

**6. Remedies**
Recipient acknowledges that any breach may cause irreparable harm to Discloser for which monetary damages are inadequate. Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies available at law or equity.

**7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**
This Agreement is governed by the laws of [YOUR STATE/COUNTRY, e.g. "the State of Washington, United States"], without regard to conflict of laws rules. Any disputes will be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in [YOUR COUNTY/CITY, e.g. "King County, Washington"].

**8. Entire Agreement**
This is the complete agreement regarding confidentiality. It may only be modified in writing signed by both parties.

**Tips for Use (delete before sending):**
- Customize the term (3–5 years is common for consultants).
- Add a "Schedule A" attachment if sharing specific items (e.g., "Access to private Git repo X").
- For international consultants: Consider adding "Recipient will comply with all applicable U.S. export control laws."
- If two-way sharing is needed (e.g., consultant shares their own IP), make it mutual by duplicating Section 1 and swapping roles.
- Track in a private spreadsheet: date signed, consultant name, services scope, expiration.
- If the consultant is overseas, note potential enforcement challenges — weigh risk vs. value.

This version adds stronger protections (reverse-engineering ban, injunctive relief, survival clause) while staying concise for early-stage use.
