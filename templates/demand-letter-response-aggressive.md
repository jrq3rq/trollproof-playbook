# Aggressive Demand Letter Response Template
TrollProof Playbook – Troll Deterrence Kit (Advanced / High-Confidence Version)

**Purpose:** Stronger, more confrontational first reply for when you have high confidence the claim is weak (e.g. obvious §101 Alice vulnerability, no marking, expired patent, or known troll).
Goal: Signal you're ready to fight, force early withdrawal, and create public-record evidence of defects.

**Important notes (delete before sending):**
- Use ONLY if you're prepared to litigate or go public.
- Tone is firm and technical — still professional, **never** rude or threatening.
- Send via email + certified mail.
- Attach **nothing** sensitive — only public links or your own public docs.
- Save everything: letter, send date, proof of delivery.
- This is **not** legal advice. If suit is threatened or stakes are high → get attorney immediately.

**Subject Line Options:**
- Formal Response & Request for Evidence – U.S. Patent No. [PATENT NUMBER]
- Demand for Claim Chart, Marking Compliance & §101 Analysis – Re: [PATENT NUMBER]

**Body:**

[Your Name / Company Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State, ZIP]
[Email]
[Date]

[Recipient Name / Law Firm]
[Their Address]
[City, State, ZIP]

Re: Your Letter Dated [DATE] Asserting Infringement of U.S. Patent No. [PATENT NUMBER]

Dear [Recipient Name or "Sir/Madam"],

We have received your letter dated [DATE] alleging that [Your Company/Product Name] infringes U.S. Patent No. [PATENT NUMBER] (the "Patent").

After preliminary review, we have serious concerns regarding the validity and enforceability of the asserted claims. To assist our evaluation, please provide the following within **14 days**:

1. A detailed claim chart mapping **every element** of each asserted independent claim to specific, publicly verifiable features of our product(s), including exact version numbers, dates of use, and supporting evidence (screenshots, source code excerpts if publicly available, user documentation, etc.).

2. Full compliance documentation under 35 U.S.C. § 287 (marking requirement), including:
   - Proof of continuous marking of the patented article with "Patent" or "Pat." plus the patent number, or
   - Evidence of substantial compliance, or
   - Sworn statement explaining why marking was not required.
   Absent proper marking, damages are strictly limited to the period after actual notice.

3. Confirmation of the Patent's current status, including:
   - Any terminal disclaimer, reissue, reexamination, IPR, PGR, CBM, or other post-grant proceeding.
   - Remaining term (expiration date).
   - Any recorded assignments or changes in ownership.

4. Analysis addressing eligibility under 35 U.S.C. § 101.
   Many software and AI-related claims have been held ineligible post-Alice Corp. v. CLS Bank (573 U.S. 208, 2014). Please provide a detailed explanation of how the asserted claims satisfy the two-step Alice framework (not an abstract idea, and if abstract, contain inventive concept/significantly more).

5. Identification of the specific accused product(s), method(s), or service(s), including version history and dates of alleged infringement.

6. Copies of the complete USPTO file history/wrapper, including all office actions, responses, amendments, and examiner notes.

Until the above materials are provided, we are unable to substantively respond or engage in licensing discussions. We reserve all rights and defenses, including but not limited to non-infringement, invalidity under § 101, § 102, § 103, and unenforceability.

We intend to defend our position vigorously if necessary.

Please direct all further communications to the undersigned.

Sincerely,

[Your Full Name]
[Your Title, e.g. Founder & CEO]
[Your Company Name]
[Email]
[Phone – optional]

cc: [Your Attorney – if consulting reactively]

**Customization Tips (delete before sending):**
- Timeframe: 14 days is aggressive — signals seriousness.
- Add patent-specific attacks if you know them:
  - Expired/near-expired: "Please confirm remaining enforceable term."
  - Known prior art: "We are aware of [public reference] that appears to anticipate the claims."
  - NPE/troll: Add nothing — let the tone and demands speak.
- Optional stronger close:
  > "Should this matter proceed, we will seek recovery of all available fees and costs, including under 35 U.S.C. § 285 if the case is deemed exceptional."

**After sending:**
- Log everything (date, method, receipt proof).
- If no reply in 30 days → high chance of bluff/weak case.
- If they send claim chart → use `alice-101-challenge-outline.md` to tear it apart.
- Consider anonymized public post (see `shame-post-template-x.md`) if they persist.

This version often ends low-effort troll campaigns quickly — they must invest real work or risk looking frivolous.

Stay strong. Stay fast.
