# Demand Letter Redaction Guide
TrollProof Playbook – Public Shame + Deterrence Loop

**Purpose:** Safely anonymize a patent troll demand letter before posting publicly (X, LinkedIn, Reddit, blogs) to expose weak claims without revealing your identity, company, or sensitive details that could backfire.

**Goal:** Maximize publicity pressure on the troll while minimizing risk to you (e.g., retaliation, doxxing, or giving them new info).

**When to redact & post**
- You’ve already sent a response (basic or aggressive template).
- Troll hasn’t dropped it quickly.
- You’re ready for visibility (many trolls fold on exposure).
- **Never** post if active litigation or credible threat of suit — consult attorney first.

**Redaction Rules – Do This Every Time**
1. **Remove all personal/company identifiers**
   - Your name, company name, product name/version
   - Your address, email, phone
   - Recipient’s name, law firm name/address
   - Any reference to your website, domain, app store link

2. **Obscure patent & claim details if paranoid**
   - Full patent number → show only last 4 digits (e.g. "US 12,345,XXX")
   - Assignee/owner name → replace with "[Redacted NPE]" or "a holding entity"
   - Specific claim language → paraphrase broadly (e.g. "using AI to personalize content" instead of exact quote)

3. **Blur or black out in screenshots**
   - Use any free tool: Preview (Mac), Paint (Windows), Markup (iOS), Photopea.com (online)
   - Black out: names, addresses, emails, phone numbers, full patent numbers
   - Leave visible: general claim wording, demand amount (if you want to show greed), "pay or sue" threats

4. **Remove dates if risky**
   - Letter date → blur or say "recent"
   - Response deadlines → generalize ("they demanded payment in 14 days")

5. **Never include**
   - Your bank info, payment demands (if any)
   - Internal notes or your drafts
   - Anything that could identify users/customers

**Step-by-Step Redaction Process**

1. Save original letter as PDF (never edit the original).
2. Make copy named "demand-letter-redacted.pdf".
3. Open in PDF editor or screenshot tool:
   - Black out all names, addresses, emails, phones.
   - Black out full patent numbers (or crop).
   - Leave claim language visible but paraphrase in post text if paranoid.
4. Export new PDF or take clean screenshots.
5. Double-check: Can anyone reverse-search to find you? (search redacted phrases)
6. Save original + redacted versions privately (encrypted drive).

**Post Checklist (before hitting publish)**
- [ ] All personal/company info blacked out?
- [ ] Patent number fully or partially obscured?
- [ ] No identifying URLs, app names, screenshots of your product?
- [ ] Tone in post neutral/professional (no insults)?
- [ ] Tagged watchdogs (@EFF, @unifiedpatents, @trollingeffects)?
- [ ] Ready to pin thread/article for visibility?

**Common Mistakes to Avoid**
- Leaving header/footer with your address
- Posting full patent number (easy to search back to you)
- Including your response letter (reveals strategy)
- Naming the troll firm publicly (they may retaliate)
- Posting before responding (loses leverage)

**2026+ Tips**
- Trolls now monitor X/LinkedIn → post anonymously if high-risk
- Use burner account for first post if paranoid
- If viral → expect troll to contact you privately (log it, don’t engage alone)

Redact carefully. Post boldly.
One clean exposure post often ends the threat.

Stay anonymous. Stay loud.
