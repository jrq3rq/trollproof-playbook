# Basic NDA Template
TrollProof Playbook – Trade Secrets Protection

**Purpose:** Simple, one-page non-disclosure agreement to protect proprietary information (code, prompts, datasets, user insights, algorithms, business logic) when sharing with freelancers, contractors, advisors, beta testers, or potential partners.

**Important notes:**
- This is **not** lawyer-drafted or customized for your jurisdiction.
- Use for low-risk, short-term sharing only.
- For high-value deals or long-term relationships, get a real attorney to review/adapt.
- Sign digitally (DocuSign, HelloSign, or print-sign-scan).
- Keep signed copies private and timestamped.

---

**NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (Basic – One-Way)**

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on [DATE], by and between:

- **Disclosing Party:** [YOUR FULL NAME or COMPANY NAME], located at [YOUR CITY, STATE, COUNTRY] ("Discloser")
- **Receiving Party:** [RECIPIENT FULL NAME or COMPANY NAME], located at [RECIPIENT CITY, STATE, COUNTRY] ("Recipient")

**1. Definition of Confidential Information**
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by Discloser to Recipient, whether orally, in writing, or electronically, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure. This includes, but is not limited to:
- Business plans, strategies, roadmaps
- Source code, prompts, AI models, training data, algorithms
- User data, insights, feedback loops
- Product designs, features, prototypes
- Financial information, pricing, customer lists

Confidential Information does **not** include information that:
(a) is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement;
(b) was already known to Recipient prior to disclosure;
(c) is independently developed by Recipient without use of Discloser's information;
(d) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

**2. Obligations of Recipient**
Recipient agrees to:
(a) Hold Confidential Information in strict confidence and not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent of Discloser.
(b) Use Confidential Information **only** for the purpose of [BRIEFLY DESCRIBE PURPOSE, e.g. "evaluating a potential collaboration" or "providing freelance development services"].
(c) Limit access to Confidential Information to employees/contractors who need to know and who are bound by similar confidentiality obligations.
(d) Protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care as Recipient uses for its own confidential information (but no less than reasonable care).
(e) Immediately notify Discloser of any unauthorized use or disclosure.

**3. Term**
This Agreement remains in effect for [CHOOSE ONE: 2 years / 5 years / indefinite] from the date of last disclosure, except for trade secrets, which remain protected as long as they qualify as trade secrets under applicable law.

**4. Return or Destruction**
Upon request or termination of discussions, Recipient will promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information (including copies) and certify in writing that it has done so.

**5. No License or Obligation**
Nothing in this Agreement grants any license, right, or interest in the Confidential Information. This Agreement does not obligate either party to enter into any further business relationship.

**6. Governing Law**
This Agreement is governed by the laws of [YOUR STATE/COUNTRY, e.g. "the State of Washington, United States"], without regard to conflict of laws principles.

**7. Entire Agreement**
This is the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter. It may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first above written.

**Tips for Use (delete before sending):**
- Customize brackets [] before use.
- For two-way protection (both sides sharing secrets), duplicate section 1 and change "Discloser/Recipient" accordingly.
- Add specifics to "Purpose" to limit scope (reduces risk of over-broad claims).
- If sharing code/data, attach a clear inventory/schedule of what's confidential.
- For international recipients: consider adding export control clause if your tech could be sensitive.
- Track all signed NDAs in a private spreadsheet with dates/signatures.

This template prioritizes simplicity and enforceability for early-stage sharing.
As your startup grows, upgrade to a full attorney-drafted version.
