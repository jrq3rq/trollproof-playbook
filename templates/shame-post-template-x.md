# Shame Post Template – X (Twitter) Version
TrollProof Playbook – Public Shame + Deterrence Loop

**Purpose:** Anonymized, viral-ready post for X/Twitter when a troll sends a demand letter.
Goal: Expose weak claims publicly → pressure troll to drop (many fold on publicity), deter others, build community support.

**Important notes (delete before posting):**
- **Anonymize everything** — redact names, patent numbers (or use partial), company details.
- Post from your personal/main account or burner if paranoid.
- Tag watchdogs: @EFF @unifiedpatents @trollingeffects @PatentTroll
- Use thread format (1/5, 2/5…) for readability.
- Attach redacted letter screenshot (blur sensitive parts).
- If troll escalates → consider deleting or pinning as evidence.
- This is **not** legal advice — consult attorney if suit threatened.

**Thread Template (copy-paste & customize):**

1/5
Another day, another patent demand letter from a non-practicing entity (NPE/troll).

They claim my [general niche, e.g. "small AI tool for education"] infringes their vague patent on "[broad claim paraphrase, e.g. 'using AI to personalize content']".

Redacted version attached.

No claim chart. No evidence. Just "pay us or else".

2/5
Quick review shows:
- Claims look highly abstract → likely ineligible under §101 Alice (mental process on generic computer/AI)
- No proof of marking under §287 → damages probably limited to post-letter
- Patent owned by [redacted NPE name or "shell entity"] — classic troll behavior

3/5
I'm a bootstrapped founder building useful tools. No big VC money. No desire to fight in court.

But I won't be shaken down by low-effort shakedowns.

So here's the public record: your move. Provide a real claim chart or drop it.

4/5
Shoutout to the community: if you've seen similar letters from [redacted firm or "this pattern"], reply/DM.

Let's crowdsource prior art and make these weaker.

Tagging @EFF @unifiedpatents @trollingeffects — thoughts?

5/5
Stay fast, stay free, fellow builders.
Don't let trolls slow you down.

Ship relentlessly.

[Attach redacted letter screenshot – blur names, full patent # if risky, keep claim language visible]

**Optional Add-Ons / Variations:**
- If patent is laughably broad:
  "Claim 1 basically says 'use computer to do thing humans have done forever'. Alice says no."
- If you have prior public use:
  "We were shipping this feature >1 year before their filing date. Prior user rights FTW."
- Call for help:
  "Anyone have prior art on [broad idea, e.g. 'rule-based content personalization'] pre-[year]? DM me."

**Posting Tips:**
- Post during peak hours (e.g. 9–11 AM PST weekdays) for max visibility.
- Use hashtags sparingly: #PatentTroll #Startup #AI #Innovation
- Pin the thread to your profile for a week.
- If responses come in → thank people, share anonymized wins.

This format often ends troll campaigns fast — publicity is their kryptonite.

Expose smart. Stay free.
