## Quick Moat Killers to Avoid
- [ ] Publicly sharing proprietary data / prompts / models
- [ ] Over-relying on one OSS library everyone uses
- [ ] Building features that big players can copy overnight
- [ ] Ignoring community — let trolls paint you as "just another clone"

Revisit this worksheet every 3 months.
The goal isn't perfection — it's compounding small moat advantages weekly until trolls see zero ROI in suing you.
