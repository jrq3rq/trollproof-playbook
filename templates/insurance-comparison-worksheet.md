# Insurance Comparison Worksheet
TrollProof Playbook – Insurance Firewall

**Purpose:** Simple, founder-friendly table to compare low-cost IP liability / cyber insurance policies that cover patent defense costs — even if you never file patents yourself.
Goal: Transfer litigation risk cheaply (~$1–5K/year) without any IP entanglements or defensive filings.

**Important notes:**
- This is **not** insurance advice or endorsement.
- Prices and coverage change — always get current quotes.
- Focus on policies that cover **defense costs** for patent infringement claims (even meritless ones).
- Get quotes from 2–3 providers before buying.
- Update this worksheet annually or after major product changes.

**Key Coverage Questions to Ask Each Provider (add notes in table):**
- Does it cover **non-practicing entity (NPE/troll)** assertions?
- Covered for **software/AI** claims? (many exclude "internet business methods")
- Defense costs **outside** policy limits? (very valuable)
- Covers **settlement** amounts or only legal fees?
- Any **prior acts** coverage? (if claim relates to old code/feature)
- Waiting period? (e.g. 30–90 days before coverage starts)
- Retroactive date? (coverage for past acts before policy start)

**Recommended Providers (2026 starting points – research current):**
- Founders Shield (startup-focused, often cheapest)
- Embroker (tech startup specialist)
- Chubb / Hiscox (more traditional but good IP riders)
- Coalition (cyber + IP combo)
- At-Bay (cyber-first, add IP endorsement)

**Quick Decision Guide**
- Budget < $2K/year → Prioritize Founders Shield or Coalition
- Need high limits ($2M+) → Look at Chubb/Hiscox
- Heavy AI/software focus → Confirm no "internet business method" exclusion
- If denied coverage → Ask why (helps refine product to avoid triggers)
