# Basic Demand Letter Response Template
TrollProof Playbook – Troll Deterrence Kit

**Purpose:** A short, polite-but-firm first reply to a patent infringement demand letter (usually from a troll/NPE).
Goal: Force them to show their cards, highlight weaknesses, and often make them drop or reduce demands without escalating to court.

**Important notes (delete before sending):**
- Customize [brackets] with real details — do NOT send blank.
- Send via email + certified mail (proof of receipt).
- Keep tone professional — no insults or threats.
- Attach nothing sensitive — only public info.
- Save copy + send date + proof of delivery.
- This is **not** legal advice. If letter threatens suit or has high stakes, consult attorney immediately.

**Subject Line Options:**
- Response to Your Patent Assertion Letter Dated [DATE]
- Request for Claim Chart and Evidence – Re: [PATENT NUMBER]

**Body:**

[Your Name / Company Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State, ZIP]
[Email]
[Date]

[Recipient Name / Law Firm]
[Their Address]
[City, State, ZIP]

Re: Your Letter Dated [DATE] Regarding Alleged Infringement of U.S. Patent No. [PATENT NUMBER]

Dear [Recipient Name or "Sir/Madam"],

I am in receipt of your letter dated [DATE], asserting that [Your Company/Product Name] allegedly infringes U.S. Patent No. [PATENT NUMBER] (the "Patent").

To evaluate your claim and respond appropriately, please provide the following information and materials within [14 / 21] days:

1. A complete claim chart mapping **each element** of at least one asserted independent claim to specific features of [Your Product Name / version]. Include citations to publicly available evidence (screenshots, source code references, user guides, etc.).

2. Identification of the specific product(s), service(s), or method(s) you allege infringe, including version numbers or dates of use.

3. Proof of compliance with 35 U.S.C. § 287 (marking requirement). Specifically:
   - Evidence that the patented article has been marked "Patent" or "Pat." with the patent number, or
   - Proof that marking was substantially complied with, or
   - Explanation why marking was not required.

   Absent proper marking, damages are limited to the period after actual notice (your letter). Please confirm the earliest date of marking or notice.

4. Confirmation whether the Patent is subject to any terminal disclaimer, reissue, reexamination, IPR, PGR, CBM, or other post-grant proceeding that may affect validity or enforceability.

5. Identification of the named inventor(s) and any assignments recorded with the USPTO.

6. A copy of the file history/wrapper for the Patent, including all office actions, responses, and amendments.

We take intellectual property matters seriously and will review your allegations carefully once provided with the above materials. Until then, we are unable to substantively respond or discuss licensing or other resolutions.

Please direct all further communications to the undersigned.

Sincerely,

[Your Full Name]
[Your Title, e.g. Founder]
[Your Company Name]
[Email]
[Phone – optional]

cc: [Your Attorney – if you have one consulting reactively]

---

**Customization Tips (delete before sending):**
- Adjust timeframe (14–21 days is standard — long enough to look serious, short enough to pressure them).
- If you suspect §101 Alice vulnerability (common for AI/software), add politely:
  > "We note that many software and AI-related claims have been found ineligible under 35 U.S.C. § 101 following Alice Corp. v. CLS Bank. Please address how the asserted claims satisfy the Alice two-step framework."
- If patent looks expired/near-expired: add request for "confirmation of current status and remaining term."
- If troll is known for quick drops after pushback: keep tone firmer but still professional.
- Never admit infringement — even hypothetically.

**After sending:**
- Log date/time sent + method (email + certified mail).
- If no response in 30 days → likely bluff/weak case.
- If they reply with claim chart → forward to attorney or use `alice-101-challenge-outline.md` to evaluate.

This basic response often kills low-effort troll letters.
They must spend real effort to continue — many don't.

Stay calm. Stay firm.
