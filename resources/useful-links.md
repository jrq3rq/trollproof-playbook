# Useful Links
TrollProof Playbook – Resources

**Purpose:** Curated, free, evergreen links to tools, databases, and communities for DIY patent scanning, prior art hunting, troll exposure, and founder defense.
All 100% free (no paid tools required). Check quarterly for updates — laws/tools change.

**Patent & Prior Art Databases (Core Scanning Tools)**
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com
  (Best for quick keyword searches + claim reading)
- USPTO Patent Public Search: https://ppubs.uspto.gov/pubwebapp/static/pages/landing.html
  (Official US database — use for full file histories)
- Lens.org: https://www.lens.org
  (Global patents + scholarly articles — great for non-US prior art)
- Espacenet (EPO): https://worldwide.espacenet.com
  (Worldwide coverage — check before international expansion)
- arXiv.org: https://arxiv.org
  (Preprints — search AI/ML/niche papers pre-filing dates)

**Prior Art & Troll Watch Resources**
- Wayback Machine (Internet Archive): https://archive.org/web
  (Archive old websites, blogs, product pages for dated proof)
- OpenTimestamps: https://opentimestamps.org
  (Free blockchain timestamping for your defensive pubs/logs)
- EFF Trolling Effects Database: https://trollingeffects.org
  (Anonymized troll demand letters — search patterns)
- Unified Patents: https://www.unifiedpatents.com
  (Free prior art database + troll watch — no membership needed for basic use)

**§101 Alice & Case Law Quick References**
- Alice Corp. v. CLS Bank (Supreme Court 2014): https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/13pdf/13-298_7lh8.pdf
  (The original ruling — read for abstract idea basics)
- USPTO §101 Guidance (AI-specific): https://www.uspto.gov/patents/laws/examination-policy/subject-matter-eligibility
  (Latest examiner instructions on AI eligibility)
- Patently-O Blog: https://patentlyo.com
  (Daily patent news — search "§101" or "Alice" for recent cases)

**Communities & Support**
- Reddit: r/patents, r/startups, r/Entrepreneur, r/MachineLearning
  (Great for prior art asks — use anonymously)
- Hacker News: https://news.ycombinator.com
  ("Ask HN" for technical prior art)
- Indie Hackers Forum: https://www.indiehackers.com
  (Bootstrapped founder stories + troll advice)

**Insurance & Firewall Options**
- Founders Shield: https://foundershield.com
  (Startup-focused IP liability quotes)
- Embroker: https://www.embroker.com
  (Tech insurance specialist)
- Coalition: https://www.coalitioninc.com
  (Cyber + IP bundles)

**Timestamp & Anonymity Tools**
- OpenTimestamps Client: https://opentimestamps.org
  (Free cryptographic proof for docs/logs/pubs)
- ProtonMail or Tutanota: https://proton.me / https://tutanota.com
  (Anonymous email for burner accounts)
